I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/). I received my B.Eng. in Biomedical Engineering from [Huazhong University of Science and Technology](https://english.hust.edu.cn/).

My research focuses on learning from neural signals and connecting them to speech and language.

- **Neural-to-Language Decoding** — Recovering speech and text from brain activity.
- **Multimodal Representation Learning** — Aligning neural, acoustic, and linguistic representations.
- **Neural Foundation Models** — Pretraining models on neural recordings for transfer across people, sessions, and tasks.
- **Large Language Models** — Studying how language models use context and prior knowledge for reasoning and generation.
- **AI Agents** — Building agents that plan, use tools, and adapt based on feedback.

I also write a [technical blog](https://jurio.blog.csdn.net/).
